Round avatar. Falls back to Hebrew initials on the brand gradient when no image is supplied — used in the topbar, customer tables, and instructor lists.

```jsx
<Avatar name="מיכל לוי" />
<Avatar name="דנה כהן" size="lg" />
<Avatar name="איתן מנהל" src="/photo.jpg" size="sm" />
```

Sizes: `sm` 28px · `md` 36px (default) · `lg` 48px. Initials take the first letter of the first two name parts.
